- `mark_order_paid_v1(uuid)` remains service-role-only; future removal or canonicalization is a separate decision.

- `SEARCH_USERS_CALLER_DRIFT` is closed as `NOT_ACTIVE_RUNTIME`: the earlier blocker was based on incorrect runtime ownership, and the stale `search_users_v1` reference was in an inactive root source copy rather than the mobile, dashboard, or web runtime.

- Version families require exact-signature caller and body comparison.
- Runtime not observed does not approve removal.
- Backend-only payment confirmation and unsafe check-in functions remain service-role only.
